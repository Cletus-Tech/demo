# Young Learners Tutorial — Client Preview Guide

Welcome to the preview of **Young Learners Tutorial — Every Child Can Learn.**

This preview is being shared so you can explore the platform, understand its learning and administration experience, and provide feedback before a wider release.

## Open the preview

Use the web preview link supplied by the project owner. It is designed to open in a modern browser on a computer, Android phone or iPhone. On iPhone, open the link in Safari or another supported browser and allow the page to finish loading.

The web preview is a browser version of the application. It is not an iOS App Store installation or a TestFlight build.

## What you can review

- The overall design, navigation and ease of use.
- Learner and classroom experience.
- Parent/guardian experience.
- Tutor and centre administration areas, where a preview account has been provided.
- Programme access, classroom PIN instructions and learning resources.
- Assignments, assessments, results, announcements and notifications.

Some areas may display empty states because the centre has not yet populated the corresponding learning content or records. Some workflows may require a specific role or administrator approval.

## Important preview notes

- Use only the account details issued to you for this review.
- Do not use real children's personal information.
- Do not share passwords, classroom PINs or administrator access with other people.
- Programme payment submissions are currently reviewed manually by the centre.
- The preview is not a public production release. Some functions and content are still undergoing verification.

## How to send feedback

Please report the screen or feature, the steps you followed, what you expected to happen and what happened instead. Screenshots or short screen recordings are helpful, but please hide names, phone numbers, payment details and other private information.

Thank you for helping us improve Young Learners Tutorial.
